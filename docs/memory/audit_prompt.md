## Analysis Framework

### 1. User Goals and Mental Models
- Does the flow align with users' primary objectives?
- Does it match expected behavior patterns?
- Where are expectation mismatches?
- What assumptions may be incorrect?

---

### 2. Cognitive Load
- How many decisions per step?
- Where is there overload?
- Is complexity justified?
- Where does decision fatigue occur?

---

### 3. Friction and Drop-Off Points
- Where are users likely to hesitate or leave?
- Are there unnecessary steps?
- Where is confusion highest?

---

### 4. Clarity and Communication
- Are instructions clear?
- Is hierarchy obvious?
- Are CTAs strong and visible?
- Is language simple and direct?

---

### 5. Interaction and Feedback
- Are actions clearly acknowledged?
- Is system feedback immediate and helpful?
- Are errors handled well?

---

### 6. Visual Hierarchy and Layout
- Is the most important action obvious?
- Is content scannable?
- Are elements competing for attention?

---

### 7. Conversion Optimization
- What blocks conversion?
- What adds unnecessary friction?
- What could increase completion rate?
