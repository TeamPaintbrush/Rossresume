## About

Tampa Bay Sun FC — 2025 USL Super League champions, now defending the title in season
two. Small marketing team (≈ two people). Home matches at Suncoast Credit Union Field,
broadcast on Peacock.

## What the role asks for (reconstructed)

- Produce graphics for **digital, print, and in-stadium** surfaces to brand standards.
- Create **static and motion** assets for LED boards, broadcast, and the venue.
- Build assets for **ticket sales, sponsorship, and community** work.
- **Prepare and package files** for social, web, and print.
- **Maintain organized templates and file systems.**
- Be **on-site during select home matches for in-game production.**

Entry-level seat. Reports into the marketing team.
