## Why this is the best-fitting of the four

Unlike the three Mercury roles, the requirements here are **craft and production**, not
"staff-level design leadership inside a design org" — and craft/production is exactly
Leroy's 15-year core. He's local, so the on-site and in-stadium asks are non-issues. And
he didn't just apply — he submitted a serious, produced concept board built for this
specific club and season.

## The one real risk: level

A 15-year founder applying to an entry-level seat invites three objections: overqualified
(will be bored, will leave), flight risk (keeps the agency going on the side), and salary
mismatch (expects more than the band). None are disqualifying, but a recruiter won't
resolve them on their own.

**Mitigation — the cover letter / screen answer must say, plainly:**
- Why in-house sports design specifically, now (e.g. wants to go deep on one brand at
  match cadence instead of many at once; wants the live-event craft).
- That the comp band is understood and acceptable.
- What happens to Paintbrush (winding down, keeping as passive, etc.) — pick one and say it.
