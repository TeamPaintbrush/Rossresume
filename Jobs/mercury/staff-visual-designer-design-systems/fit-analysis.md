## The unfair advantage

The JD explicitly wants React front-end prototyping, working with engineers on how systems
get built in code, and AI-assisted prototyping. That's rare in a visual designer and it's
precisely his profile. The `what-if-it-worked` format-breakdown docs + the Magnific
parameter guide + the Style Registry are direct evidence of "build the systems, guidelines,
and references that make craft repeatable."

## Why it still doesn't clear the bar

Staff visual design at Mercury means being the person other designers' type and color
decisions get measured against. Gated on 10+ years of deep typography/color/iconography
craft **and** a mentoring track record — neither is in the codebase or résumé.

## Recommendation

Apply only after the Accounting Integrations role. If applying: lead with the
"designer who codes and ships the system" angle, use the breakdown docs as proof of
repeatable-craft-systems thinking, and be honest about the type/icon-depth gap rather than
papering over it.
