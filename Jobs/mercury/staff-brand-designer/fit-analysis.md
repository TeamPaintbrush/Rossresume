## Strengths to lead with

- **AI in the creative process** — the JD dedicates a whole bullet to it. The AI label
  generator, fine-tuning on label sets, LLM routing, and the "don't render text in the
  image model, do a separate overlay pass" discipline are a more concrete AI-in-craft
  story than most brand designers bring.
- **Systems thinking** — zone-based template frameworks, the Style Registry ("every case
  study must use a distinct style direction, tracked").
- **Tenure** — 15 years creative; the numeric bar is met.

## Gaps that block

- **Pedigree** — they explicitly want "agency, fashion, elevated consumer, or
  design-forward tech." History is small-business packaging/marketing.
- **Surface area** — campaigns, web, social, events, editorial typography, motion. The
  portfolio is packaging/label and product renders. This is not a packaging role.
- **Figma design-system ownership** — different thing from React component systems to them.
- **Team leadership** — mentoring IC3/IC4 through critique and pairing; no evidence.

## Recommendation

If this were a scored screen, likely filtered before portfolio review on pedigree +
surface-area mismatch. Paths in: (1) a portfolio that shows genuinely elite
typographic/editorial/campaign work, or (2) a referral who can vouch for craft.

Better-fit target: brand/packaging design lead at a CPG or beverage company, or a
design-technologist / AI-design role where the tooling work is the point.
